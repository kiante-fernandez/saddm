# Verification

| script | what it does |
|---|---|
| `parameter_recovery.py` | 100-config NUTS recovery study: parameters drawn by Latin hypercube at s = 1, data generated by the exact inverse-CDF sampler, fitted with numpyro. `OUT_DIR` / `SHARD` / `N_SHARDS` env vars support sharded cluster runs; results checkpoint per config and resume. |
| `recovery_figure.py` | Posterior-vs-truth panel for all seven parameters. |
| `compare_to_fortran.py` | Per-subject scatter and k-sweep curves against the Fortran benchmark (`data/itc_amasino/`). |

The analysis scripts read the shipped reference results
(`results/reference/`) by default and regenerate the published figures and
numbers exactly; point `RESULTS` at a fresh run to analyze a reproduction.

## Conventions

- Everything is at diffusion coefficient **s = 1**; `a` is the full boundary
  separation and `sa`/`st`/`sz` are full widths of the uniform across-trial
  distributions. Ratcliff-convention (s = 0.1) values convert by ×10 on
  a, v, sv, sa.
- Recovery data comes from `sample_ddmsa_exact` (inverse-CDF of the analytic
  density), not Euler simulation: Euler overshoots the boundary and inflates
  recovered `a` and `sv` enough to masquerade as recovery failure.
- The recovery model samples non-decision time by its lower edge
  (`t_edge ~ U(0, min RT)`, `t = t_edge + st/2`); bounding `t` itself by
  min(RT) excludes the truth whenever `st > 0`.
- In `fit_ddm_itc.f90` output the start point is mirrored
  (`z_fortran = 1 − z`); `compare_to_fortran.py` accounts for this.

## Headline results (shipped in `results/reference/`)

Recovery over 100 datasets (500 trials each): a/z/v/t at r = 0.93–0.98 with
near-nominal coverage; sv/sa/st at r = 0.65–0.71, with non-convergence
confined to extreme true `sv`. Per-subject ITC fits reproduce the Fortran
benchmark at r = 0.95 (value coefficient); the population k-sweep tracks the
Fortran curve on identical trial samples.
